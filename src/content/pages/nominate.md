These awards are designed to recognize and honor women who have made extraordinary, recognizable contributions within the communities we serve. Each year, through the Women of the Year program, SWAN provides the opportunity to recognize multiple award recipients in the categories of Professional Achievement, Mentorship of Women, Transformative Leadership, and Community Engagement, as well as celebrating the Rising Swans in the next generation of leaders. Additionally, SWAN may recognize one Lifetime Achievement award recipient. These honorees have dedicated their lives to serving others.

## Eligibility

The nominee must live or work in Skagit County and actively contribute to the community they represent. The nominee must be able to attend the SWAN Woman of the Year Banquet & Awards Ceremony, which is typically held on the third Thursday in October at the Swinomish Casino & Lodge from 5 to 8 pm.

## Nomination process

In an effort to further the empowerment of women in Skagit County, SWAN has eliminated the interview process and is, instead, requiring nominators to provide additional detail and supporting information to assist the SWAN Board of Directors and Selection Committee in their review and decision-making processes.

All nominees attend the Nomination Celebration in August. At this event, all nominees receive a certificate of recognition for their contributions to our community. The SWAN Board also announces the Rising Swan award recipients and the four Woman of the Year award recipients (without disclosing their respective category). The Rising Swans, Lifetime Achievement, and four Woman of the Year recipients attend the Woman of the Year Banquet in October to accept their awards in their respective category and to be celebrated for their contributions to our community.

SWAN accepts nominations year-round and will contact nominators and nominees annually by August 10th.
