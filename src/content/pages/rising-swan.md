---
title: "Rising Swan"
subtitle: "Recognizing the next generation of leaders in Skagit County."
---
Celebrate National Business Women's Week during the third week of October by nominating a dedicated woman for the Rising Swan awards. These awards are designed to recognize and honor women who have made extraordinary, recognizable contributions within the communities we serve.

A Rising Swan nominee is emerging and growing in her professional achievement, mentorship of women, community engagement, and transformative leadership endeavors.
