---
title: "Lifetime Achievement"
subtitle: "Honoring women who have dedicated their lives to serving others."
---
Celebrate National Business Women's Week during the third week of October by nominating a dedicated woman for the Lifetime Achievement award. These awards are designed to recognize and honor women who have made extraordinary, recognizable contributions in the areas of Professional Achievement, Mentorship of Women, Transformative Leadership, and Community Engagement.

A Lifetime Achievement nominee has dedicated her life to her professional achievements, mentorship of women, community engagement activities, and transformative leadership endeavors.
