Each year, the SWAN Scholarship is awarded to women who work or reside in Skagit County. SWAN's goal is to award a minimum of $2,000 to two or more recipients each year.

Applications are accepted year-round, with consideration occurring annually by September and fund distribution occurring as early as October or as late as August of the following year, determined by the recipient. To apply, please complete the application form below.
