The Skagit Women's Alliance and Network is a non-profit organization dedicated to recognizing and supporting women of achievement in Skagit County, and to serve as a network liaison between various women's organizations in the Skagit Valley. We also provide annual scholarships for women mentors so that they may continue the legacy of leadership and excellence.

Interested in joining the SWAN board of directors? Download the application below and email it to us.

## Other volunteer opportunities

Not ready for a board seat? We always need helping hands for the Nomination Celebration in August, the Women of the Year Banquet in October, and the Legacy Celebration in March. Send us an email with your availability and interests and we will match you with one of our projects.
