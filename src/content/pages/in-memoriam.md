---
title: "In Memoriam"
subtitle: "Honoring the legacy of the women of SWAN."
---
As we reflect upon the vibrant tapestry of the women who have been part of the Skagit Women's Alliance and Network community, it is with heavy hearts that we pay tribute to the remarkable women who have played an instrumental role in shaping its landscape. In this In Memoriam, we remember and honor the cherished lives of those who have left an indelible mark on our hearts and in the history of SWAN.

These remarkable women were not just professionals; they were visionaries, trailblazers, and pillars of strength. Their dedication, passion, and relentless pursuit of excellence have left an enduring legacy that continues to inspire future generations. Whether leading successful enterprises, mentoring aspiring leaders, or spearheading philanthropic endeavors, their contributions have left an indelible mark on Skagit County's economic and social landscape.

As we mourn the loss of these esteemed women, let us also celebrate the spirit that defined their lives. Through the stories we share and the lessons we've learned, their spirit lives on, inspiring us to continue the work they started. May their legacies endure as a source of inspiration for generations to come.
