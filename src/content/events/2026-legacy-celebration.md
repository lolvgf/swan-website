2024 was Skagit Women's Alliance and Network's 40th Anniversary of honoring and celebrating the outstanding women in Skagit County. To celebrate the Women of SWAN, the Skagit Women's Alliance and Network held its 1st Legacy Celebration Event on International Women's Day. Each March, SWAN hosts its Legacy Celebration to celebrate the Women and Friends of SWAN.
